I am a Ph.D. candidate in Construction Science at Texas A&M University, advised by Dr. Youngjib Ham. I design and evaluate human-centered teleoperation and robotic systems that improve safety, situational awareness, and efficiency in construction.

M.S. Computer Science, Texas A&M  ·  B.S. Architectural Engineering, Seoul National University

Research Areas

🤖

Human-Robot Interaction

Developing advanced telerobotic control frameworks with intuitive interfaces for construction applications, focusing on spatial awareness and operator safety.

👁️

Computer Vision

Applying computer vision algorithms and deep learning for automated construction site monitoring, activity recognition, and safety management.

🎮

Teleoperation Systems

Designing and evaluating human-centered visualization interfaces and haptic feedback systems for remote construction equipment operation.

🏗️

Construction Automation

Integrating robotics, virtual reality, and sensory feedback technologies to enhance construction productivity and workplace safety.

Featured Research

Selected studies on how visual and haptic feedback can make remote construction operations safer and more intuitive.

Haptic Feedback

End-to-End Framework for Proximity-Based Preemptive Haptic Feedback in Safe Excavation

Automation in Construction, 167, 106961

Delivers haptic cues to the operator ahead of a potential collision, based on the measured proximity between the excavator and nearby hazards.

Visual Interface

Divergent Effects of Visual Interfaces on Teleoperation for Challenging Jobsite Environments

Automation in Construction, 167, 105683 · 2024

Compares visual interface configurations under challenging jobsite conditions, showing that their effects on performance and workload differ by environment, with implications for user-centered interface design.
